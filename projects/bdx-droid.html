BD-X Style Droid

Just starting

This project is just getting started. The goal is a BD-X-style droid: a small, expressive bipedal character robot in the style of Disney's BD-X.

My plan is to use custom electronics reused from a Unitree Go2.

Locomotion will be trained with reinforcement learning in NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab, with the aim of carrying the policies from simulation to the real robot. More here as it develops.
