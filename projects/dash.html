Dash — Brushless Motor Driver & Electrical Stack

Motor Control

Dash is a high-power brushless motor driver built for a new humanoid robot platform, rated at 48 volts and 70 amps continuous — enough to drive the large actuators needed for full-body humanoid motion.

In addition to the motor driver, I am designing the broader electrical stack for the robot, including power distribution, communication backplane, and sensor integration.

Dash communicates over CAN-FD, giving the robot's high-level compute a fast, reliable link to the motor drivers and sensor network.
